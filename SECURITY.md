# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in this site or its source code, please report it responsibly.

**Do not open a public GitHub issue.** Public disclosure of a live vulnerability puts visitors at risk.

### How to report

Email: <!-- TODO: Replace with your professional email --> **kranthi@YOUR_DOMAIN.com**

Include in your report:
- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce (proof of concept if possible)
- The potential impact
- Any suggested remediation

### What to expect

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of your report | Within 48 hours |
| Confirmation of the issue (or explanation if not reproducible) | Within 7 days |
| Patch or mitigation shipped | Within 14 days for critical issues |
| Public disclosure (coordinated with reporter) | After patch is live |

### Scope

In scope:
- The live site and its Cloudflare Worker backend
- The source code at [github.com/nagulapallikranthi/enterprise-agile-site](https://github.com/nagulapallikranthi/enterprise-agile-site)

Out of scope:
- Cloudflare infrastructure (report to Cloudflare directly)
- Denial of service attacks
- Issues requiring physical access to systems
- Social engineering of the site owner

### Recognition

There is no bug bounty programme. However, responsible disclosures will be credited publicly (with your permission) once the issue is resolved.

### Good-faith expectations

This site operates under a good-faith vulnerability disclosure policy. Reporters who follow responsible disclosure practices will not face legal action. Reporters who publicly disclose vulnerabilities before a patch is available may void this protection.

*This policy follows the [CERT Coordination Center's responsible disclosure guidelines](https://www.kb.cert.org/vuls/govdisclosure/).*
